Proposed framework


Domain experts write fairness definitions in logical systems of non-binary truth values.

Example: probabilities $P(A\cap B) = P(A)\cdot P(B)$ for disjoint $A,B$

Convert to semi-structured text

Example:
   bias MEANS NOT interesting
   interesting MEANS A AND B

• Abstract terms A,B, "interesting", "bias"
• Starting point of social engagement
• Stakeholders agree on meaning

Equivalent numeric formulas

Equivalent example:
   $P(bias) = 1-P(interesting)$
   $P(interesting) = P(A)\cdot P(B)$

• Terms are abstract statements
• Questionnaires & interviews gather truth values on opinions

Option: fuzzy logics


Several variations, three base ones.

• $a=μ(A) =$ truth value of A
• $a \Rightarrow b=μ(A\text{ IMPLIES }B)$
• $a \star b=μ(A\text{ AND }B)$ for independent A,B (actually called conjunction instead of AND)

Gödel logic

Models worst case

$a\star b = \min(a, b)$

$a\Rightarrow b= \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } a \le b \\ b & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$

Product logic

Models probabilities

$a\star b = a \cdot b$

$a\Rightarrow b= \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } a \le b \\ \frac{b}{a} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$

Łukasiewicz logic

Models predicate logic

$a\star b = \max(0, a + b - 1)$

$a\Rightarrow b=\min(1, 1 - a + b)$
 

Fuzzy logics


Different degradation of truth values.
Assumption: discrimination opposes indistinguishibility between groups.

discrimination E(A,B) MEANS OPPOSITION TO indistinguishibility(A,B) indistinguishibility(A,B) MEANS (A IMPLIES B) AND (B IMPLIES A)

Gödel logic

$(0.5 \Rightarrow 0.3)=0.3$

$μ(E)=1-\min(a,b)$

Product logic

$(0.5 \Rightarrow 0.3)=0.6$

$μ(E)=1-\min(\tfrac{a}{b},\tfrac{b}{a})$

Łukasiewicz logic

$(0.5 \Rightarrow 0.3)=0.8$

$μ(E)=|a-b|$

• Choose a different logic for each thresholding strategy!
• Thresholds = added assumptions to logic definitions!
